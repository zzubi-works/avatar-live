Refund Policy#

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Refunds are not available#

Avatar Live is a downloadable digital product.

  • No refunds are given after purchase.
  • Due to the nature of digital products, withdrawal or refunds within a set period after purchase (for example, 14 days) also do not apply.
  • No refunds are given for a change of mind, failure to check your environment, not having an avatar project, compatibility problems with other programs, avatars or shaders, or choosing the wrong edition.

Please check before buying#

  • [ ] Is your PC running Windows 10 / 11 64-bit? (macOS and Linux are not supported)
  • [ ] Do you have a graphics card that supports DirectX 11?
  • [ ] Do you have the Unity project of the avatar you want to use (Unity 2022.3.22f1, in a state that uploads to VRChat normally)? VRM or .vrca files alone cannot be used.
  • [ ] Do you have a regular color webcam?
  • [ ] Can you connect to the internet for activation and license checks?
  • [ ] Have you checked the supported features?
  • [ ] If you will stream or use it commercially, did you choose Pro? (Editions)

Payment and refund inquiries#

Payment is made through BOOTH (pixiv Inc.). Please direct inquiries about payment and refunds to BOOTH directly, not to the seller. The seller has no means of processing refunds.

If something does not work#

Check Troubleshooting, and if that does not solve it, let us know through the contact channels in Seller Information.
