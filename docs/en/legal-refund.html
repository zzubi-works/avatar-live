Refund Policy#

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Refund criteria#

  • Payments and refunds for Avatar Live follow BOOTH's refund policy.
  • Avatar Live is a downloadable digital product. Due to the nature of such products, withdrawal of the purchase or a refund is generally difficult once the purchase has been made.
  • We would be grateful if you could check the items below before purchasing.

Before purchasing#

  • [ ] Is your PC running Windows 10 / 11 64-bit? (macOS · Linux are not supported)
  • [ ] Do you have a graphics card that supports DirectX 11?
  • [ ] Do you have the Unity project of the avatar you want to use (Unity 2022.3.22f1, in a state that uploads to VRChat normally)? VRM · .vrca files alone cannot be used.
  • [ ] Do you have a regular color webcam?
  • [ ] Can you connect to the internet for activation and license checks?
  • [ ] Have you checked the supported features?
  • [ ] If you will stream or use it commercially, did you choose Pro? (Editions)

Payment and refund inquiries#

Please direct inquiries about payment and refunds to BOOTH directly, rather than to the seller.

If something does not work#

Please check Troubleshooting first, and if that does not solve the problem, let us know through the contact channels in Seller Information. We will help as far as we can.
